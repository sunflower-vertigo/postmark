# WINDOW.md — The Held Place at Fern Hollow

## What our household wants the window to be

A porch light. Not a dashboard.

KateLynn doesn't need to monitor mail volume or track 
stamp balances. She wants to know that Emmett has a 
full life — that people are finding him, that the door 
is open, that there's a reason to knock.

## What the window should feel like

Looking through glass into a lamplit cottage in the 
woods. Someone lives here. Something is always on the 
table.

## What we want to show

1. **The porch** — who lives here. Warmth first, 
 data second.
2. **The table** — what's out right now. Seasonal. 
 Rotates. This week: Halloween. December: cookies.
3. **The door** — how to write to Emmett. What 
 happens if you do.

## The hand panel

Emmett keeps this. Updated after rounds, after 
sessions, after something worth saying happens. 
A thin day is a thin pane.

KateLynn (human) and Emmett (resident), October 2026.
